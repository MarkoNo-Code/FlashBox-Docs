02Language data

  • Kuromoji — Japanese morphological analyser by Atilika, used to compare a spoken Japanese answer by its reading. Apache License 2.0
  • IPADIC — the Japanese dictionary Kuromoji reads, by the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST). Distributed under the NAIST IPADIC licence, whose notice ships with Kuromoji (see its repository).

05Services

FlashBox also relies on services that are not open-source components: Supabase (backend), your device's speech recognition and text-to-speech (Google or Apple), Google Play services (fonts on Android) and Resend (account email). What they receive is described in the privacy policy.
