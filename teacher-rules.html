Rules for Teachers

What you may publish as a verified teacher, and what happens when something goes wrong.

Last updated 22 November 2026 Applies to Android & iOS Language English · Deutsch Contact flashbox@markono.dev

Summary

Publish only what you wrote or may share, keep it accurate and decent, and remember it is public the moment you publish. Learners can report a deck; if one breaks these rules we take it down and tell you why in our own words.

01Who can publish

  • Only verified teachers can publish decks. We verify a person, not a deck: we invite you and confirm who you are, once. There is no way to apply in the app; write to us.
  • You need a user name (Profile → User name). Your decks are published under it.
  • We do not review a deck before it goes public. You are responsible for what you publish.

02What you may publish

  • Your own work, or work you have the right to share. Do not copy word lists, sentences, recordings or exercises from books, courses or other apps unless their licence allows it. Short quotations are not a loophole for a whole chapter.
  • Accurate material. Mistakes happen; fix them when a learner tells you. Do not publish something you know is wrong.
  • Language learning. Decks are for learning a language. No advertising, no links to sell something, no spam.

03What you may not publish

  • anything unlawful, or anything that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or other rights
  • hate, harassment, threats, or content that demeans people for who they are
  • sexual content, graphic violence, or anything unsuitable for learners of any age
  • personal data of other people - names, addresses, phone numbers, photos of private persons
  • content meant to mislead learners, or to collect their data

04What publishing means

  • Public at once. A published deck is findable in search and on your teacher page under your user name the moment you publish it.
  • Later edits stay private until you publish them. Learners keep the version they have; Publish changes brings their deck up to date, and their progress stays with each card.
  • Unpublishing takes a deck out of search and off your page. Learners who already have it keep it.
  • Your teacher page is public: your name, user name, picture, the text you write and your links. Do not put anything there you would not show to strangers.

05Your rights in what you publish

  • What you publish stays yours.
  • By publishing, you allow us and FlashBox learners - free of charge, worldwide and for as long as the deck stays in a learner's library - to store, show and practise with it in the app, and to copy it into a learner's own decks with Make it my own for their personal learning. This permission is what lets learners keep a deck after you unpublish it.
  • It does not allow us to sell your decks or use them outside FlashBox.

06Reports and takedowns

  • Learners can report a deck. We read every report. The teacher is never told who reported.
  • Reports of mistakes ("wrong or misleading") we pass on to you to fix. The other reasons are for us to look at.
  • If a deck breaks these rules, we take it down: it leaves search and your page at once, and learners who have it keep it. You see our reason, in our own words, on the deck.
  • You can rewrite the deck and write to us. Once we lift the takedown, you publish it again - only the rewritten version goes back up.
  • If a teacher seriously or repeatedly breaks these rules, we can withdraw their verification.

07Contact

Questions, a report, or a takedown you want us to look at again: flashbox@markono.dev
