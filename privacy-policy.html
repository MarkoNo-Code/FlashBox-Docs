Privacy Policy

FlashBox — a language-learning app for Android and iOS in which you answer flashcards by speaking. This policy describes what the app stores, what happens to your voice, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 22 November 2026 Applies to Android & iOS Language English · Deutsch Contact flashbox@markono.dev

Summary

FlashBox stores your account, the decks and cards you create, and your learning progress. What you say is turned into text to grade your answer; the recording itself is never stored and never reaches our servers. There are no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell data.

02What data we collect

Account

  • Guest account: you can start without signing up. The app then creates an account with no email address and no password; it is identified by a random id only.
  • Email account: if you sign up, your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash by our backend provider; we cannot read it.
  • Google or Apple account: if you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider confirms who you are and passes us an identifier, your email address and, if you share it, your name. With Apple you can hide your email address; we then receive an address at Apple's relay service, which forwards our mails to you. We receive no password.
  • Profile: the display name you choose, the language you know, and your settings in the app (for example hands-free mode and the colour of each language).

Content you create

  • Decks and cards: the words, phrases, readings and example sentences you type or import. They are private to your account.
  • User name: if you choose one. It is visible to others only if you are a verified teacher.
  • If you are a verified teacher: the decks you publish, and your teacher page - your name, user name, picture, the text you write and your links - are public to every FlashBox user. We also count how many learners have each of your published decks and show you that number, never who they are.

Learning progress

  • Review state: for each card you have practised, its box, its next due date and your score per exercise.
  • Session history: when you practised, which cards were asked, whether each answer was right, how long you took, and the text the speech recognizer heard for each spoken answer.

Feedback and reports

  • Feedback: if you send us feedback from the app, the text you wrote, the app version and your platform. It is stored without your account: we cannot see who wrote it and cannot reply.
  • Card reports: if you report a card from our catalogue, the reason you chose, your optional note, which card and deck it was, the text the speech recognizer heard for that answer, the grade and the exercise. It is stored without your account as well.
  • Deck reports: if you report a teacher's deck, the reason, your optional note and which deck. This one is stored with your account, so that you can report a deck only once; the teacher never learns who reported.

Technical data

  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a report with the stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version and a random installation id. It contains no account id, no email address and nothing you said or typed.
  • Server logs: like any online service, our backend provider logs requests, including IP address and time, for security and troubleshooting.

FlashBox does not collect your location, your contacts, your photos or an advertising id, and it contains no advertising or analytics software.

03Your voice

Speaking is how you answer in FlashBox, so this is the part worth reading.

  • Recognition is done by your device's speech service — on Android the speech recognition service installed on the phone (usually Google's), on iOS Apple's. Depending on the language and your device settings, the audio is processed on the device or sent to that provider to be transcribed. Their privacy policies apply to that step.
  • FlashBox receives only the transcribed text. The text is compared with the card's answer and stored in your session history, so the app can show what was heard.
  • No recording is stored or uploaded by FlashBox. If you switch on "Hear yourself", your last answer is kept in memory to play it back to you once and is then discarded. It never leaves the device through us.
  • The microphone is open only during a practice session and only while the session screen shows it as open.
  • Reading aloud — the voice that reads a card's answer — is your device's own text-to-speech voice.

04App permissions

Microphone

Purpose Hearing your spoken answers during a practice session. On iOS the system asks separately for speech recognition, for the same purpose.

Usage Only while a practice session is open. You can practise without it only in exercises that do not need speech.

Internet

Purpose Signing in, loading your decks, saving your progress, and the speech service's own connection.

Usage FlashBox keeps no offline copy of your decks; it needs a connection to work.

05How we use your data

  • Running the app: showing your decks, grading your answers and scheduling each card's next review.
  • Your statistics: the charts and figures you see in the app are computed from your own session history.
  • Account mail: confirming your email address, and changing it or your password when you ask.
  • Fixing faults: crash reports tell us where the app failed.
  • Improving how answers are judged: we may look at how close heard texts were to the expected answers, in aggregate, to tune the point at which an answer counts as right.

We use your data for nothing else: no advertising, no profiling, no sale.

The legal bases under the GDPR are the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) for running the app and your account, and our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in a stable and secure app for crash reports and server logs.

06Service providers

We do not sell your data. It is processed by these providers, and only to run the app:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in. Your account, decks, cards and progress are stored in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Resend — delivery of account emails (confirmation, password and email changes). Receives your email address and the message.
  • Google and Apple - sign-in, only if you choose it. On Android, signing in with Apple opens a page of Apple's in the browser. Their privacy policies apply to that step.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports, as described above.
  • Google or Apple speech services — transcription of your spoken answers, as part of your device's operating system.
  • Google Fonts (Android) — the fonts for Japanese and Thai text are fetched through Google Play services on first use.

Data is also disclosed where the law requires it.

Note on transfers

Your learning data is stored in the EU. Resend, Google and Apple may process data outside Switzerland, the EEA and the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Such transfers are made under the providers' data processing terms and the safeguards they contain (standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision).

07How long we keep it

  • Account, decks, cards and progress: until you delete them or your account.
  • Guest accounts: the same. A guest account you no longer use stays stored until you delete it in the app or ask us to; a guest account that was never used for practice and has not been opened for 2 months is deleted automatically, with everything stored in it.
  • Feedback and card reports: until we have dealt with them. Because they are stored without your account, they are not removed when you delete your account - and cannot be traced back to you.
  • Deck reports: until we have dealt with them, and with your account at the latest.
  • A teacher's published decks: learners who have one keep it after it is unpublished, as the rules for teachers describe. Deleting the teacher's account deletes them.
  • Crash reports: 90 days, the period set by the provider.
  • Server logs: for the short period our backend provider keeps them.

08Data security

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row Level Security in the database: every request is limited to the rows of the signed-in account
  • Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on the device and excluded from device backups
  • No payment data is collected

09Your rights

This policy is written to comply with:

  • The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP)
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the EU and EEA
  • The UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 for users in the United Kingdom
  • Google Play and Apple App Store requirements

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: information about the data stored about you
  • Rectification: correction of incorrect data — most of it you can change in the app yourself
  • Erasure: deletion of your data
  • Data portability: your cards can be exported from the app (deck menu, "Export cards"); for anything else, write to us
  • Objection and restriction: objecting to, or limiting, the processing of your data
  • Complaint: lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority — Switzerland: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC/EDÖB, edoeb.admin.ch); EU/EEA: your local supervisory authority; UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)

To exercise any of these rights, write to flashbox@markono.dev.

10Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and all data that belongs to it at any time.

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account, then confirm. This also works for a guest account.
  • By email: if you can no longer open the app, write to flashbox@markono.dev from the address of your account.

Deleted with the account: your sign-in, profile, decks and cards, review state and session history. See the account deletion page for details.

Important notice

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. There is no grace period.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains a feature. The current version is always on this page, with the date of the last change at the top.
