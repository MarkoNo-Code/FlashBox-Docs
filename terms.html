Summary

FlashBox is for your personal learning. Your decks stay yours; ours are for use in the app. The app is provided as it is, the speech recognition can mishear you, and you can leave at any time by deleting your account.

01What FlashBox is

FlashBox is a language-learning app for Android and iOS. You practise flashcards by saying the answer out loud, and the app schedules each card's next review. It is provided by Marko Nonninger, Winterthur, Switzerland ("we").

These terms apply when you use the app. How your data is handled is described separately in the privacy policy.

02Your account

  • You can use FlashBox as a guest or with an account that has an email address and a password.
  • Keep your password to yourself. What happens under your account is your responsibility.
  • You may choose a user name. It must not impersonate someone else or FlashBox, and must not be offensive.
  • A guest account exists only on the device it was created on. If you remove the app or lose the device, its decks and progress cannot be recovered. Adding an email address keeps them.

03Using the app

  • FlashBox is for your personal, non-commercial learning.
  • The app is currently free of charge. If paid features are ever added, you will be told the price before you can be charged for anything.
  • The app needs an internet connection; it keeps no offline copy of your decks.

04Your decks and cards

  • They stay yours. Decks and cards you write or import belong to you. They are private to your account - unless you are a verified teacher and publish a deck, which the rules for teachers cover.
  • What you allow us to do with them: store and process them as far as needed to run the app for you - nothing more.
  • Imported material: if you import a deck someone else made (for example an Anki deck), you are responsible for being allowed to use it.
  • Export: you can export your cards from the app at any time.

05Our catalogue

  • The decks FlashBox ships ("the catalogue") are ours. You may use them in the app for your own learning, and you may copy a catalogue deck into your own decks and change the copy.
  • You may not republish, sell or redistribute the catalogue or substantial parts of it.
  • Decks from teachers are written by verified teachers, not by us, and are not reviewed before they are published. They carry the teacher's user name. You may use them in the app for your own learning, and copy one into your own decks. If something in one is wrong or should not be there, report the deck from its menu.
  • The catalogue is written with care but may contain mistakes, and not every language has been reviewed by a native speaker. Do not rely on it where a mistake would matter - a legal, medical or official text, for example.

06Speech recognition and grading

  • Your spoken answers are transcribed by the speech service of your device (Google's or Apple's). We do not control that service and cannot promise it hears you correctly.
  • The app's verdict on an answer is a learning aid, not an examination. It can be wrong in both directions.
